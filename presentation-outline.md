## 3. Customer challenge and scope

- First-time shoppers need a clear route from parking to the right store
- Deliver a main-level Mappedin wayfinding application with search and step-by-step directions
- Keep the first release focused on the visitor journey

## 4. What we delivered

- Deployed Mappedin wayfinding experience with search, Browse, route previews, and directions
- 134 verified storefronts and 150 named main-level locations
- Simulated walking, arrival offers, Food Court Menus, and Events
- React, TypeScript, Bootstrap, TanStack Query, Docker Compose, and Nginx

## 5. What we deliberately did not build

- No speculative backend, checkout, analytics, notifications, or account system
- No claim of real-time indoor positioning; the blue dot is clearly simulated
- No claim that blueprint-based routes have been physically commissioned yet
